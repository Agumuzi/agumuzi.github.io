What guides the work我坚持的开发方式

A few decisions that make software easier to trust, use and maintain.

让软件更易使用、维护，也更值得信赖的几个原则。

01

Local-first where practical尽可能本地优先

Keep data and essential workflows within the user's control.

在可行的情况下，让数据和关键工作流掌握在用户手中。

02

Modular by design明确的模块边界

Clear parts are simpler to replace, test and repair.

使系统更容易替换组件、测试与定位问题。

03

Iterate in real conditions在真实使用中迭代

Observe friction, make a change, then verify it helps.

从实际问题出发，改进之后再验证是否真正有效。
